Albiqo

Your privacy stays with you

Albiqo has no server of its own and no advertising or analytics SDKs. It does not send us your photos, videos or search content. Access to Apple services and exports you initiate are explained below.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Photos permission

Only photos and videos you allow through iOS can be read. Limited access restricts results to those items. Change or revoke access in iPhone Settings.

On-device smart search

Duplicate analysis, image comparison and text recognition run on your device. Text and image/face similarity data may be saved locally for search. Person similarity is an experimental suggestion, not identity verification.

iCloud and internet use

Cleanup scans do not download content from iCloud. In the swipe game, upcoming images may load from Apple iCloud over suitable Wi-Fi; cellular connections show only locally available images. Downloads you explicitly start for preview or export may use internet data. Apple manages iCloud syncing.

Information saved on your device

Scan results and progress, selections, list positions, game decisions, last album choice and cleanup totals are saved in the app’s storage. Deleting Albiqo from your iPhone removes its local data; albums in Photos and files exported elsewhere remain. Your iOS backup settings also apply.

Albums and originals

Albums are created in Apple Photos. Adding items puts them in the target album and removes them from other editable user albums; it does not delete the library file. Compression and resizing create new copies and preserve originals.

Deletion and storage statistics

Deletion applies to your Photos library after iOS confirmation. If iCloud Photos is on, deletion may sync to your other devices. Items normally stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days. Shown MB totals reflect measurable content sizes; actual immediately available space can differ because of iCloud, caches and Recently Deleted.

ZIP files and sharing

USB ZIPs remain in Files → On My iPhone → Albiqo until you delete them. A trusted computer can access them through Apple Devices. Apps or destinations you choose for sharing have their own policies. Originals may include metadata such as date and location; exporting does not anonymize them.

Trial and lifetime license

The free trial lasts 24 hours from activation through Apple. There is no automatic charge or subscription. Afterwards, you can buy a lifetime license with one payment. The purchase screen shows the current price in your storefront’s currency. Restore purchases using the same Apple Account.

Apple StoreKit

Trial and purchase transactions are verified through Apple StoreKit. Albiqo does not receive card or bank details. Apple’s payment, account and privacy terms also apply.
